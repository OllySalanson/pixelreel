# Privacy

*Draft: not final yet.*

Pixel Reel keeps as little as it can, for as short a time as it can.

## What it keeps

- **Your reel:** the scenes Claude sends to make it (titles, captions, the words to say and
  the pictures), the recordings of the voice, and the video if you ask for one.
- **Nothing about you:** there are no accounts, so no names or email addresses.
- **For a short while, where requests come from:** each caller's internet address is counted
  in the computer's memory for up to a day, to stop anyone making too many reels. It is not
  written down, and it is gone when the service restarts.

## How long

Reels are deleted after 30 days. Ask Claude to delete a reel and it is gone at once, with its
recordings and video.

## Who can see a reel

Anyone with its link. Links are long and random, so nobody can guess one, but anyone you
send a link to can watch, and send it on. Do not put anything in a reel you would not want
seen by whoever might get the link.

## Who else is involved

- The voice is recorded on Pixel Reel's own computer, with an open speech model. Your words
  are not sent to a voice company or any other outside service.
- Claude, which writes the scenes, is run by Anthropic, under Anthropic's own privacy policy.
- Pixel Reel runs on a hosting company's computers, which store the reels for it.

## This website

- It uses no cookies, no trackers and no adverts, and loads nothing from anywhere else.
- If you change how it looks in Make it comfy, that choice is kept on your own device. It is
  never sent to Pixel Reel.
- The box that writes what to say to Claude does its work on your device. What you type in
  it is not sent to Pixel Reel.
- Where the box says Make my reel, it makes the reel for you. Then what you give it (your
  words, a link, a file or a photo) is sent to Pixel Reel, and on to Claude, to write the
  reel. Pixel Reel does not keep what you gave it: only the reel.

Press kit

Pixel Reel, in brief

The short version, the facts, and pictures you are welcome to use.

In one line

Pixel Reel turns anything wordy into a short cartoon, read to you in a warm voice.

In a paragraph

Pixel Reel is a free add-on for Claude. Ask Claude to explain something, such as a letter, an article or a plan, and it comes back as a pixel reel: a short pixel-art cartoon with one idea in each scene, big plain captions, and a warm narrator called Peter.

It is made for people who find reading hard work, such as people with dyslexia or ADHD. And for anyone who would simply rather watch.

The facts

What it costs
Nothing. It is free.
What you need
Claude. Any plan, the free one included.
How long a reel is
Up to twelve scenes, and under four minutes. Most run for one or two.
How long it takes
About as long as the reel itself.
The voice
Peter: a computer voice built from public domain audiobook readings. It cannot be made to copy anyone.
Privacy
No accounts. Only people with a reel's link can watch it. Reels are deleted after 30 days. The voice is made by Pixel Reel itself, not by a voice company.
The code
The player and its tools are open source, under the MIT licence.
Where it is now
Early days. By invitation, for now.

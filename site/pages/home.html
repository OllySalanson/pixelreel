ANNUAL BENEFIT STATEMENT FOR THE SCHEME YEAR ENDING 5 APRIL 2026

Dear Member,

We are writing to provide you with your Annual Benefit Statement for the above Scheme year, as required under the Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Disclosure of Information) Regulations 2013. This statement sets out the value of your accumulated fund as at the statement date, the contributions received during the Scheme year, and a Statutory Money Purchase Illustration of the benefits which may become payable to you at your Selected Retirement Age.

As at the statement date, the value of your accumulated fund, after the deduction of the annual management charge and any applicable transaction costs, was £18,204.37. During the Scheme year, member contributions of £1,512.00 and employer contributions of £1,134.00 were received and invested in accordance with your current investment instructions, or, where no instructions are held, in the default lifestyle arrangement.

The illustration overleaf is prepared in accordance with the relevant actuarial technical memorandum and is expressed in today's prices, on the assumption that contributions continue at their present level until your Selected Retirement Age and that future price inflation is 2.5% per annum. The illustration is not guaranteed. The benefits ultimately payable will depend upon the contributions actually paid, the investment returns achieved (which may be negative), the charges deducted, and the cost of securing an income at the point of retirement, and may therefore be higher or lower than the amounts shown.

Please check the personal details recorded in Section 1 and notify the Scheme Administrator in writing of any discrepancy, quoting the reference above. You are not required to take any further action in respect of this statement, which should be retained with your records. Further information regarding your options at retirement, including the availability of free and impartial guidance, is set out in Sections 4 to 7.

Questions

The ones people ask most.

Does it cost anything?

No. It is free.

Do I need Claude?

Yes. Claude writes the scenes, so you need a Claude account. The free plan works.

How long does it take?

About as long as the reel itself. So, a minute or two.

How long can a reel be?

Up to twelve scenes, and under four minutes. Short is the whole point.

Can it get things wrong?

It can. The words come from Claude, and from what you give it. For money, health or the law, check with whoever sent you the original.

Can I keep my reel?

Yes. Ask Claude for the video. You get a file you can save, or send on WhatsApp.

Is there an app?

Yes, of a kind. Add Pixel Reel to your phone's home screen, and it opens like one. Show me how.

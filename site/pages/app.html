You have it. This is Pixel Reel, on your home screen.

How to add it

Two presses. That is all.

Find your phone here. If the big button did it for you, you are already done.

On an iPhone or iPad
  1. {{sprite:share:3}}

    Press Share

    The square with an arrow, in Safari. It may be hiding behind three dots.

  2. {{sprite:addhome:3}}

    Press Add to Home Screen

    It is a little way down the list. Then press Add.

On an Android phone
  1. {{sprite:dots:3}}

    Press the three dots

    At the top of Chrome.

  2. {{sprite:addhome:3}}

    Press Add to Home screen

    Then press Install.

After that, Pixel Reel is in your phone's Share menu. Reading something long? Press Share, then Pixel Reel.

On a computer
  1. {{icon:computer:3}}

    Look in the address bar

    Chrome and Edge show a small Install button at the end of it. Press it.

  2. {{icon:phone:3}}

    Or use your phone

    Point its camera at this square. This page opens there. Then press the big button.

# Credits

Pixel Reel stands on the shoulders of some generous people.

## The voice

- **[Pocket TTS](https://github.com/kyutai-labs/pocket-tts)** by Kyutai is the speech model
  that records Peter. Its code is released under the MIT licence, and its model under
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- **Peter's voice**, `peter_yearsley`, comes from
  [Voice-Zero](https://github.com/OwenTyme/voice-zero)'s selection of public domain LibriVox
  readings (CC0).

## The letters

- **[Lexend](https://www.lexend.com/)**, the letters this site is set in, was made to be easy
  to read. By Bonnie Shaver-Troup, Thomas Jockin and the Lexend Project Authors.
- **[Atkinson Hyperlegible Next](https://www.brailleinstitute.org/freefont/)**, by the Braille
  Institute, and **[OpenDyslexic](https://opendyslexic.org/)**, by Abbie Gonzalez, are the
  other letters you can pick in Make it comfy.
- **Orange Kid**, the pixel letters in the reels, is by Ray Larabie of
  [Typodermic Fonts](https://typodermicfonts.com/) (CC0).

The first three are released under the
[SIL Open Font License](https://openfontlicense.org/).

## The code

- **[pixelreel](https://github.com/OllySalanson/pixelreel)** is the open-source player and
  tools behind it all, under the MIT licence. This website is in there too.

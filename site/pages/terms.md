# Terms

*Draft: not final yet.*

Pixel Reel is free, and offered as it is.

- **Use it for good.** Do not make reels that break the law, deceive people, harass anyone,
  or pretend to be a real person or organisation. The narrators are computer voices, and
  Pixel Reel cannot be made to copy anyone else's voice.
- **Your reels are yours.** You are responsible for what is in them. Pixel Reel does not
  claim them.
- **Check what matters.** A reel explains things simply, and the words come from Claude and
  from you. For money, health, legal or safety matters, check the details with whoever sent
  you the original.
- **No promises.** Pixel Reel may be slow, may be down, and may delete a reel early if it
  breaks these terms. It keeps reels for 30 days at most.
- **Fair use.** There are limits on how many reels anyone can make in an hour, so it stays
  free for everyone.

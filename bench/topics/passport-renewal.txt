Renew or replace your adult passport (web page)

You can renew your passport online. It costs £88.50 online, or £100 with a paper form from the Post Office.

Allow 3 weeks
It usually takes up to 3 weeks to get your new passport. It can take longer if we need more information from you. Do not book travel until you have a valid passport.

What you need
- a digital photo of yourself (you can take one on a phone, with someone's help, against a plain light wall)
- a credit or debit card
- your old passport

Your photo
Your photo must be clear and in focus, with nothing covering your face. Do not smile or tilt your head. No glasses.

Sending your old passport
After you apply, you must send your old passport to us. We will tell you where to post it. Your application will not start until we get it. We will send the old one back to you, cancelled.

If your passport has been lost or stolen
You must report it first, and you cannot renew it online the normal way: you must apply for a replacement.

Urgent travel
If you need a passport within 3 weeks, you can pay for the Fast Track service (1 week, £178) or the Online Premium service (same day, £222.50), by booking an appointment.

A message from your bank about scams

We have seen a rise in phone scams where criminals pretend to be from our fraud team.

How the scam works
You get a call, a text or an email that seems to come from us. The number on your phone may even look like ours. The caller says your account is at risk and that you must move your money to a "safe account" straight away to protect it. They may already know some details about you, such as your name and your address.

What we will never do
We will never ask you to move money to a safe account.
We will never ask for your full PIN or your online banking password.
We will never send someone to your home to collect your card.
We will never ask you to read out a code we have sent you by text, so that someone else can use it.

What to do if you get a call like this
Hang up. Do not trust the number on your screen.
Wait five minutes, or use a different phone, so the line is clear.
Call us on the number on the back of your card.
If you think you have given away details or moved money, call us at once. You can also call 159, which connects you safely to your bank.

Remember: a real bank will never rush you. Take your time. It is fine to say no.

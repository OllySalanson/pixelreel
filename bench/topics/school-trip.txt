Year 4 trip to the Science Museum - Friday 11 December 2026

As part of our topic on electricity, Year 4 will visit the Science Museum. The coach will leave school at 8.45am sharp, so children must be in their classroom by 8.30am. We will return at about 3.45pm, a little after the usual end of the day. Please collect your child from the main hall.

Cost
The trip costs £14.50 a child, which covers the coach and the workshop. Please pay on ParentPay by Friday 27 November. If you receive free school meals, the trip is free, and your child will be given a packed lunch by the school.

What your child needs
- a packed lunch in a bag they can carry, with no glass bottles and no nuts
- a waterproof coat
- school uniform
- no money, phones or toys

Helpers
We need four parent helpers. If you have a DBS check with the school and can help, please tell the class teacher by 27 November.

Medicine
If your child needs an inhaler or other medicine, make sure it is in school, labelled with their name, before the trip.

Please fill in the consent slip on ParentPay. Your child cannot go on the trip without it.

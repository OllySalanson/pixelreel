How to restart your broadband hub when the internet stops working

1. First, check whether it is just one device. If your phone works on Wi-Fi but your laptop does not, restart the laptop instead.
2. Look at the light on the front of the hub. Blue means it is working. Orange or flashing orange means it has lost its connection.
3. Turn the hub off at the power button on the back, or at the wall socket.
4. Wait a full 30 seconds. This lets it clear its memory.
5. Turn it back on. The light will flash for 2 to 3 minutes while it reconnects. Do not turn it off again while it flashes.
6. When the light is steady blue, try the internet again.
7. Still not working? Check the cable from the hub to the phone socket on the wall is pushed in firmly at both ends.
8. If the light stays orange for more than 10 minutes, there may be a fault in your area. Check our service status page on your phone's mobile data, or call us on 0330 000 1234.

Do not press the small pinhole reset button unless we ask you to. It wipes your Wi-Fi name and password, and every device would need setting up again.

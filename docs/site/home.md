# Pixel Reel

Watch it instead of reading it.

A pixel reel is a short cartoon that explains one thing. Each scene has one idea, a big
plain caption, a picture, and a warm recorded voice that reads it to you. It is made for
anyone who finds a page of words hard work, such as people with dyslexia, or anyone who is
simply tired.

## How to get one

Ask Claude. Add Pixel Reel to your Claude once, then ask for something like *"Make me a pixel
reel explaining my new boiler's timer."* Claude writes the scenes, Pixel Reel records the
voice, and the reel plays right there in the chat, with a link you can send to anyone.

[Add Pixel Reel to Claude](/claude)

## Good to know

- It is free.
- Reels are kept for 30 days, and only people with the link can watch them.
- The voice is made on Pixel Reel's own computer. Your words are not sent to anyone else.
